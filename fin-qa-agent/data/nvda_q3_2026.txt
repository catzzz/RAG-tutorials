NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA) — Q3 FY2026 Earnings Summary (synthetic data for demo)

NVIDIA reported record third-quarter fiscal 2026 revenue of $51.2 billion, up 68% year over
year, driven overwhelmingly by Data Center revenue of $43.7 billion. Demand for the new
Blackwell-generation GPUs continued to outpace supply, with management noting that supply
constraints are expected to ease in the second half of fiscal 2027.

Gross margin was 74.1%. The Gaming segment contributed $3.6 billion, up 12% year over year.
Management guided Q4 revenue to approximately $54 billion, plus or minus 2%.

NVIDIA highlighted growing demand from sovereign AI initiatives and enterprise customers
building inference infrastructure, which it expects to be a multi-year growth driver.
