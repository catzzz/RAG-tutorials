Apple Inc. (AAPL) — Q3 FY2026 Earnings Summary (synthetic data for demo)

Apple reported third-quarter fiscal 2026 revenue of $94.8 billion, up 7% year over year.
Growth was driven primarily by the Services segment, which reached an all-time high of
$27.3 billion, up 14% year over year. iPhone revenue was $42.1 billion, roughly flat.

Gross margin expanded to 46.8%, up from 44.5% a year ago, helped by the higher mix of
Services. Management guided to high-single-digit revenue growth for Q4, citing strong
demand for new AI-powered iPhone features launching in the fall.

The company returned $28 billion to shareholders during the quarter through dividends and
buybacks. CEO commentary highlighted that the installed base of active devices hit a new
record across all geographic segments.
