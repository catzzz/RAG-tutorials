Tesla, Inc. (TSLA) — Q3 FY2026 Earnings Summary (synthetic data for demo)

Tesla reported third-quarter 2026 revenue of $26.1 billion, up 3% year over year, below
analyst expectations of $27.4 billion. Automotive gross margin (excluding regulatory
credits) compressed to 14.2%, down from 17.9% a year ago, due to price cuts in China and
Europe aimed at defending market share against rising EV competition.

The Energy Generation and Storage segment was a bright spot, with revenue up 52% year over
year to $4.3 billion. The company deployed a record 9.1 GWh of energy storage.

Tesla reaffirmed its target of starting volume production of its next-generation lower-cost
vehicle platform in the first half of 2027. Free cash flow for the quarter was $1.2 billion.
